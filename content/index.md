## How I work

I always start with numbers. What works and what doesn’t is often hidden behind a large amount of data that no one actually looks at, as in the [[sequence|Sequence - Finding the leak]] - where I discovered that the `first_open` event was lying to us and created a false perspective that FTUE worked correctly.  
I also think like a game & economy designer by analyzing core systems, mechanics, and features. As you can see in [[landlord|Keeping a finite world playable]] - where I had to create systems that targeted new players and allowed them to play effectively from day 1.

### Decision-making process

I decide based on cohorts and measurements before/after - and I can tell what this doesn’t prove and the limitations of this method.  
This is obviously not a perfect approach, but given the specifics of companies that I worked in and limited resources that was the way to go.
I don’t hide from responsibilities. When we lack enough data to make an informed decision, I am able to make a judgment call.

One of the games I had to kill was really dear to me, as it was my first project created entirely in Unity - [Food Truck Tycoon](https://foodtrucktycoon.com/).

### Me, myself and AI

I use LLMs every day - mostly for coding. I don’t use it to create features or economies, just tools that help me make them better. I found out that designing features with LLMs actually slows me down and creates tunnel vision that might hurt the product in the long run. I’m not an engineer, and I’m not pretending to be, but I can get quite far with prototyping systems or query analytics without including other team members. I always start with a `spec.md`.

Even creating this site, I had issues with Mermaid in Quartz; going step by step, I’ve verified that Mermaid was crashing on a global theme initialization. So it would have required diving into Quartz global theme adjustments and I’ve decided to go to `mermaid.live` and create it myself and use a `.png` in one of the case studies.  
You can check some of the tools I’ve created with Claude Code in [[tools|tools section]].
